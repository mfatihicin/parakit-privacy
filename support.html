Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Parakit has no account and no servers — your financial records never leave your phone. This also means we cannot recover data if you delete the app, so keep your own backups.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Settings → Data → Back up data to save a file, and Restore data to load it on another device. You can also import transactions from a CSV file.

I bought Premium but it's not active on my new phone.

Open the Premium screen and tap "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you used to buy it. Premium is a one-time purchase tied to that store account.

How do I remove ads?

Purchase Parakit Premium (a one-time payment). This permanently removes all ads and unlocks premium features.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Reminders are local to your device. You can manage them in your phone's system settings under Parakit → Notifications.

Can I track gold, currency, and savings?

Yes — use the Track → Assets tab. Gold can be tracked by quantity (grams, coins, bracelets) with an optional current unit price, and currency by amount.

How do I lock the app?

Settings → Security → App Lock enables Face ID / fingerprint / device passcode on launch.
